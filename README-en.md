# Android-app "Mental Counting"

Main features:
1) Infinite math problem solving
2) Solving math problems on time (15 seconds, 30 seconds, 1 minute)
3) Solving linear equations from one variable in integers

Functions:
* Saving the result offline in the local profile 
* The ability to log in via Email or Google Account to transfer the result between devices
* Difficulty level selection in each mode:
  * Easy level: numbers from -10 to 10
  * Average level: numbers from -100 to 100
  * Difficult level: numbers from -1000 to 1000

Train, become smarter, upgrade yourself
